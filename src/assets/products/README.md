# Product photos

Drop a photo here named after the product id and it is used automatically on the shop grid, product page,
bag, search and checkout. No code changes needed. Products without a photo keep their drawn illustration.

Accepted: .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif. Square or 4:5 portrait works best, at least 1200 px on the long side,
on a plain or softly lit background.
