# Image prompts: origin landscapes

Use the same settings for all six so they look like one set: portrait 4:5, at least 1200 x 1500,
golden-hour light, natural colour grade, no people, no text, no logos. Keep the lower third quiet
(mist, water, foliage) because the origin name is printed over it.

Shared suffix to add to every prompt:
"photorealistic travel photography, 35mm, golden hour, soft haze, rich warm tones, no people, no text, vertical 4:5"

cambodia.jpg
Misty forested slopes of the Cardamom Mountains in Cambodia at sunrise, layered ridgelines in warm haze, tall
tropical trees in the foreground, a few young agarwood (Aquilaria) trees with glossy leaves on a hillside.

india.jpg
Tea gardens and jungle along the Brahmaputra river valley in Assam, India at dawn, a wide slow river with mist,
low sun glowing through the haze, dense green hills in the distance.

laos.jpg
Green karst hills and a quiet river in northern Laos in the Annamite foothills, morning mist between the ridges,
a traditional wooden longboat on still water, soft gold light.

vietnam.jpg
Coast and forested mountains of Khanh Hoa, Vietnam near Nha Trang, a calm bay below green hills with low cloud,
warm early light on the water, lush tropical foliage in the foreground.

thailand.jpg
Tropical hillside forest near Trat and Chanthaburi in eastern Thailand, rows of young agarwood plantation trees
under tall canopy, shafts of light through humid air, deep greens with warm highlights.

indonesia.jpg
Rainforest canopy and a winding river in Kalimantan, Borneo, Indonesia at golden hour, mist rising from the
trees, a distant longboat on the water, dense layered jungle.
