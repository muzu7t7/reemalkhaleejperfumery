# Origin location photos

Drop one photo per origin here, named in lowercase. They are used automatically on the home page
("Six origins, six personalities") and the Oud guide origin cards. Origins without a photo keep the
current gradient look.

Files: cambodia, india, laos, vietnam, thailand, indonesia  (.jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif)

Size: portrait 4:5 or 3:4 works best (the home cards are tall arches; the guide crops to 4:3, so keep the
subject in the middle). At least 1200 px wide. Keep the bottom third calm: text is laid over it.
