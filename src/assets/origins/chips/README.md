# Oud chips photos (optional)

The Oud guide shows real oud chips on each origin card. By default it uses the matching Oud Wood product photo
(Cambodia, India, Laos, Vietnam, Thailand). Indonesia has no chips photo yet, so it shows the drawn chips.

To use your own, add a square photo (at least 600 x 600) named after the origin, in lowercase:
cambodia, india, laos, vietnam, thailand, indonesia  (.jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif)

Photo brief: a small pile of dark, resinous oud chips on a plain warm background or a brass dish, soft side
light, close and sharp, no text. Vary the colour per origin: Cambodia golden-brown and honeyed, India very dark
and oily, Laos light and warm, Vietnam near-black with glossy resin, Thailand pale and clean, Indonesia
medium brown and dry.
